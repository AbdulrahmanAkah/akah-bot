# RD16-A Daily Forensic Closure Methodology

RD16-A is a frozen diagnostic stage based on RD15 commit `85bafd02e5cee0e62c9b28d0e42dc985d8e35b82`.

It measures bull-market upside capture, regime exposure, trade concentration, exit attribution, asset contribution, score calibration, rejected-opportunity outcomes, and same-bar stop cases. It does not access 2025 or 2026, optimize parameters, or select a winner.

The full methodology and result reports are regenerated deterministically by `scripts/research/run_rd16a_daily_forensic.py`.
