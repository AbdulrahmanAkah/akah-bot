# RD16-PIT-A0 Methodology

A0 audits the frozen RD16 universes and the registered 567-trade V3 ledger.
Weekly market-cap decisions use Monday snapshots with a one-day information lag.
Stablecoins, wrapped assets, and network-specific duplicate representations are
excluded before ranking.

A0 does not rerun V3. Each frozen trade is labeled with historical rank,
Top-6/Top-10 membership, and locally documented tradability. Unresolved evidence
remains unresolved. Bounded sensitivity is diagnostic only. Moving-block
bootstrap is not a formal gate and Benjamini-Hochberg is not applied.
