# Universe-First Research Invariant

This invariant applies to every future strategy research line in the repository.

1. Point-in-time universe membership is audited before signal design, parameter
   research, optimization, or production evaluation.
2. A current survivor list cannot be projected backward into historical tests.
3. Every membership decision must identify the source metric, observation
   timestamp, availability timestamp, exclusions, aliases, and ranking rule.
4. A full validation claim requires at least 95% causal universe coverage.
5. Any run below that threshold is labeled restricted or diagnostic.
6. Restricted or diagnostic runs cannot authorize production.
7. Manual historical snapshots across distinct regimes must pass before a
   multi-year engine run.
8. Universe-size and hysteresis sensitivities are pre-registered and cannot be
   selected by realized return.
9. Addition, removal, and interaction effects are separated through factorial
   or Shapley attribution.
10. Data from 2025 and 2026 remains sealed for this research sequence.

This invariant is an empirical project rule following two major universe-driven
failures: M05 and RD16/V3.
