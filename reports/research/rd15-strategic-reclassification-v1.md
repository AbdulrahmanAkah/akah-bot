# RD15 Strategic Reclassification

RD15 remains technically valid but is rejected as the primary trading architecture for the project.

Its daily signal and execution design captured too little of the 2020–2021 bull-market opportunity, depended heavily on a small number of large winners, and did not establish reliable score ranking in transfer evidence.

RD15 is frozen as a benchmark and source of reusable engineering components. RD16-B restores the intended architecture: 1H signal and execution, 4H structure, and 1D/1W context.
