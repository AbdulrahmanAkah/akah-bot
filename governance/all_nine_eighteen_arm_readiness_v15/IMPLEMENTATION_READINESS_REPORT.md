## Prospective changes, not retrospective doctrine certification

The full source rules are frozen in `research_protocol.json` before historical
source-only scanning. In particular:

- Wyckoff uses an explicitly declared mechanical PS/SC/ST grammar and a prior
  measured bearish structural objective, not a fabricated boolean SC certificate.
- Elliott uses an explicit consecutive-pivot finite grammar, not a hidden
  outcome-selected search cap or best-count selection. Exact native subdivisions
  and all valid opposing interpretations remain required.
- Dow is no longer falsely funded via Classical or context-only aliases. It has
  its own source-bound secondary-low stop and standalone entry/owner contract.
- A historical entry lease may expire without deleting the original already
  admitted campaign. Origin integrity is checked at the original entry clock;
  explicit mutation/revocation still fails closed. Current management and adds
  require their own live current evidence.
- Harmonic actual closure can occur between owner-degree closes. A partly
  pre-completion owner bar cannot qualify as a post-completion Type-II retest.

These declarations permit an approximate AKAH research test. They do not resolve
all possible discretionary school interpretations or promise better profits.

Source-only preflight exposed two concrete implementation errors before
certification: simultaneous high/low pivots could reach range construction with
non-ordered observations, and identical shared-market facts across distinct
assets could collide in a source claim ID. The former now records a native
ordered-swing rejection instead of aborting the provider; the latter uses exact
source lineage namespaces without using asset identity as an admission rule.
Both have dedicated regression fixtures. Failed/superseded attempts were not
used as readiness evidence; the final evidence binds the final source version.

## Clock, financial and execution contract

Only completed 1H / 4H / 1D facts are consumed. Pivots are 2-left/2-right and
unavailable until the second right close. Entries are at the next 1H open.
No current-hour high/low/close/volume is exposed to entry selection.

Inherited current-MTM limits: 0.5% thesis risk, 2.5% portfolio stop-risk,
90% gross, 18% per asset, five distinct assets. Staged fills share original
campaign B0. Capacity is 0.5% of the complete trailing 24H quote-turnover proxy.
Round-trip costs are 0.25% at 1X and 0.50% at 2X. Decimal quantity normalization
uses the explicit prospective research contract; it is not historical exchange
rule certification. A stop is not a guarantee against gap/slippage loss.

Every actual source emission is sealed and linked through immutable retained
receipts: source → producer → emission → selector → fill → owner → exit →
accounting. A dictionary with a valid-looking system name cannot bypass the
real producer or source guard. Preview cannot consume live source authority.

All funded entries require a complete current PIT market context. Missing
member/leader data means UNKNOWN and abstention, never truncation of membership
or carry-forward of old bullish confirmation. Held-source gaps fail closed.

No outcome PnL, MFE, MAE or future economic lookup was used. No model fit,
parameter search or threshold optimization occurred. Synthetic price paths are
test fixtures, not market outcomes. The source-only scan includes authorized
pre-2024 rows, never fresh 2024/2025 rows or a whole-file read/hash of mixed-year

## Subsequent replay and saved output

`scripts/research/integration_v15/runner.py:execute_authorized` refuses this
preparation task. It requires a different active governed task:
`AKAH_SINGLE_FROZEN_ALL_NINE_GATE3_REPLAY_V15`, explicit user authorization and
the exact precommit SHA. Default runner invocation is readiness verification.

When separately authorized, it runs one arm at a time with actual event-driven
selection/execution, not the old V5 replay overlay. Each arm saves its full
campaign/fill/equity/decision/receipt result and accounting metrics. Qualification
uses complete 2022/2023 daily calendars and the frozen nine-claim procedure.
No incremental-role or ablation benefit is claimed without a matching test.
Campaign tables retain original immutable entry bindings rather than recycled
position IDs, all entry/add/sale fees, B0, terminal holdings and exact closure.
Separate annotated fill ledgers preserve raw receipt-bound fill bytes and record
OPEN/CLOSE phase. Complete daily equity and annual accounting are saved.
An arm's technical failure is retained with an explicitly uncertified partial
trace; the other independent arms are still attempted. Any missing/failed arm
blocks qualification of the entire frozen claim family; it is never dropped or
retuned. This continuation behavior is tested with mocks only, without consuming
a market iterator or executing any fill.

The last permissible complete mark is 2023-12-31 23:00 UTC. The last hour ending
in 2024 is explicitly unavailable under the sealed-data rule. No terminal mark
or hypothetical liquidation cost is fabricated to fill this boundary.
